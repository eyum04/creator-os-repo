# Learnings

Ongoing notes from dogfooding CreatorOS as the primary user. Honest about what the PRD got right, what it got wrong, and what I'd change if I were scoping v1 again.

This is a working document. Entries get added or revised as I use the app longer and as user interviews surface new information.

## The shot list builder may be scoped for the wrong user

I built the shot list feature because I make cinematic content myself, and I assumed other creators would want the same level of pre-production structure. Using v1, I've noticed I'm often the only one who would realistically touch this feature. A lot of creators — even ones who script — don't plan specific shots and angles ahead of time. They film what comes to mind when the camera is on.

This doesn't mean the feature is wrong. But it may be scoped for a narrower user than the PRD implied. The shot list should probably be optional, not a core pillar of the product. Something to pressure-test in user interviews.

## The calendar works, but it's missing the feedback loop

v1 lets users schedule a post date on the calendar, but there's no notification or reminder when that date approaches. This is a real gap I've felt myself — I'll schedule something, then forget about it until I open the app again. The calendar solves the "plan" problem but not the "show up" problem.

Posting reminders were listed in the PRD as a Pro feature, but after using the app, I think they belong in the free tier. Without reminders, the calendar is a passive artifact. With them, it becomes accountability. Accountability is core to the product's value proposition.

## The pipeline is doing its job

The feature I open most is the idea pipeline. Being able to see at a glance where every video is — Idea, Scripted, Filmed, Posted — is genuinely changing my content habits. I'm posting more consistently and with more intention because the pipeline shows me what's in motion and what's stalled.

This is the feature that's earning its place in v1. The pipeline is the product. Everything else is supporting infrastructure around it.

## Pillars are static; they should be more active

Pillars are the feature I open the least. Once they're set up, there's not much to do with them besides tag ideas or edit the pillar list. They're a taxonomy, not a tool.

The PRD framed pillars as a core organizing spine, and they are — but the feature itself is underbuilt. Pillar balance analytics were in the PRD as a Pro feature. After using v1, I think something lightweight and free (like a visual indicator of which pillars are underrepresented in the pipeline or calendar) would make pillars feel alive rather than dormant.

## What's next

These observations are from me, using my own product. They aren't a substitute for external validation. Next step is running user interviews with other creators to see which of these patterns hold up, which are specific to my workflow, and which reveal problems I haven't noticed yet.
